LAB 02 · VELOCITY KINEMATICS

Jacobian and singularities

The Jacobian maps joint angular velocities into end-effector velocity. At a singularity, its determinant becomes zero.

J = [ −L₁ sin θ₁ − L₂ sin(θ₁+θ₂), −L₂ sin(θ₁+θ₂) ]
  [ L₁ cos θ₁ + L₂ cos(θ₁+θ₂), L₂ cos(θ₁+θ₂) ]
det(J) = L₁L₂ sin θ₂
